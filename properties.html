01 The residences

Designed around
the view.

Ten floors. Twenty-four apartments. Two, three and four-bedroom layouts, each opening to a balcony.

AVORA sits between Gampaha and Yakkala, close enough to the town and far enough to feel quiet. The building is planned for light, cross-breeze and privacy, with a restrained palette of timber, stone and glass.

At its crown, a rooftop deck holds an infinity pool, a landscaped sky garden and a gym. Residents share a lounge, secure parking and 24/7 security, with kids' and adult pools at podium level.

The development is delivered by Sahan Constructions and brought to market by Sahan Homes. Construction is expected to begin in early 2027 and complete in 2029 — a quarter-century of delivery behind it.

Apartments from LKR 24 million. Payment plans on request.

05 The developer

Sahan Homes.
A Sahan Constructions company.

Housing projects and apartments, backed by the group's engineering and construction teams.

Sahan Homes was formed inside Sahan Constructions to focus on housing and apartments — the part of the market where the group's building experience matters most to individual buyers.

It handles land and feasibility, design coordination, construction and handover, with the same contracting standards used on the group's public and commercial projects. AVORA Residences is its first development; further sites are under review.
