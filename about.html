01 Our story

A construction company
that kept building.

A core purpose and a standard of delivery that have held across five sectors.

For twenty-five years, Sahan Group has not asked to be believed on promise alone. We have earned our standing the hard way.

Priyantha Godegama founded Sahan Engineering & Construction in 2001. From the beginning the company took on civil and building works for both public and private clients — the kind of projects where the details are inspected and the programme is fixed.

That approach carried the group through two decades of building: factories and warehouses, schools, banks, water treatment infrastructure, defence facilities and homes. In 2018 the group added its first non-construction business, Pure Spring (Green Lanka Eco Science and Technologies), bottling natural mineral water from a deep aquifer in Bandarawela.

Hospitality followed with Kaze Restaurant & Pub in Gampaha. More recently the group extended into trading with Cassandra, registered in Dubai, and into financial services with Invest Lanka. Sahan Homes, formed under Sahan Engineering & Constructions, brings the group's building experience directly to homeowners and apartment buyers.

Today the Group serves as a foundational umbrella brand — anchoring operating ventures, specialised developments and partner ecosystems with a shared standard of reliability. More than 100 people work across the five sectors.

Our purpose: to build enduring value across spaces, hospitality and everyday essentials.
