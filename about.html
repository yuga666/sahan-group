02 About us

Calm by name.
Considered by nature.

Sahan is a versatile group of companies spanning food & beverage, hospitality and real estate — one group, three disciplines and the properties they shape.

01 Our story

A group that grew
from good rooms.

From a table, to a stay, to a building — each chapter of Sahan began with the same question: how should this feel?

The name Sahan means calm — a composure we try to bring to everything we build.

Sahan began with a simple observation: the experiences people value most are rarely the loudest ones. A well-run room, a menu that respects its ingredients, a building that feels right the moment you step inside — these are made with patience, not haste.

That idea carried us from food & beverage into hospitality, and from hospitality into real estate. Each new vertical grew out of the one before it, and each is held to the same expectations: care in the detail, respect for place, and a long horizon.

Today the group operates across three verticals in Sri Lanka, with AVORA Residences marking our next chapter in residential development.

Our businesses sit close to daily life. That is exactly why the details matter.

02 Our path

Chapters, not
a straight line.

Each vertical taught us something the next one needed. Read in order, they explain how the group thinks.

01

Food & Beverage

It started at the table. Restaurants, bars and cafés taught us how much a room, a rhythm of service and a single well-made dish can do. This is still where our standards are set.

02

Hospitality

From service to stays. Hospitality extended those lessons beyond the table: rooms, villas and stays where the guest experience is engineered quietly, in the background.

03

Real Estate

Building the places we had been designing. Real estate brought everything together — land, architecture, construction and the long-term responsibility of a building. AVORA Residences is our expression of that in residential form.

04

What's next

Growing with intention. We are exploring new sites and new ideas, but we are not in a hurry. Each project has to earn its place in the group.
