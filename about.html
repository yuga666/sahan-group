02 About us

Twenty-five years.
Built on trust.

Sahan began in 2001 as a construction company in Gampaha. Today it is a diversified group of seven businesses, still run by the same standard.

01 Our story

A construction company
that kept building.

Respect, transparency and collaboration — the three values that have carried the group from its first site to seven companies.

Sahan started with a simple proposition: take on difficult work, finish it properly, and let the client's confidence bring the next project.

Priyantha Godegama founded Sahan Engineering & Construction in 2001. From the beginning the company took on civil and building works for both public and private clients — the kind of projects where the details are inspected and the programme is fixed.

That approach carried the group through two decades of building: factories and warehouses, schools, banks, water treatment infrastructure, defence facilities and homes. In 2018 the group added its first non-construction business, Pure Springs (Green Lanka Eco Science and Technologies), bottling natural mineral water from a deep aquifer in Bandarawela.

Hospitality followed with Kaze Restaurant & Pub in Gampaha. More recently the group extended into trading with Cassandara, registered in Dubai, and into financial services with Invest Lanka. Sahan Homes, formed under Sahan Construction, brings the group's building experience directly to homeowners and apartment buyers.

Today Sahan employs more than 100 people across seven companies.

Pure Springs — Green Lanka Eco Science and Technologies — begins bottling from a 115-metre aquifer, with a fully automated line in Bandarawela.

Kaze Restaurant & Pub brings Chinese and Sri Lankan dining, VIP rooms, karaoke and a banquet hall into the group.

Trading

Dubai, and a finance arm

Cassandara, registered in Dubai, handles imports and exports — fresh produce at its core. Invest Lanka extends the group into loans, gold loans and vehicle leasing.

Sahan Homes is formed under Sahan Construction to handle housing projects and apartments. AVORA Residences in Gampaha is its first development.
