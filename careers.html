01 Life at Sahan

Hire for temperament.
Train for craft.

We look for people who notice the details and want to get them right — then give them room to grow.

Sahan should be a place where people can move across verticals, learn a second craft and take on more than their job title suggests.

We are a group built by people who stay. Our kitchens, front desks and project teams are small enough that your decisions show up in the room, the service or the building — and supported enough that you are not left to guess.

If you care about the details and prefer building over announcing, you will recognise the culture here. We invest in training, promote from within wherever we can, and measure good work by what guests and residents experience.
