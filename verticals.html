03 Verticals

Three verticals.
One standard.

Food & beverage, hospitality and real estate — distinct businesses, held to a shared idea of quality.

01 The businesses

Different rooms, kitchens and construction sites. The same expectations.

Each vertical has its own rhythm and its own team. Food & beverage moves nightly; hospitality is measured in seasons; real estate is measured in decades.

What connects them is a way of working: care in the detail, respect for place, and a long horizon. Our businesses are run separately, but they are held to one standard — and they learn from each other.

01Food & Beverage 02Hospitality 03Real Estate Enquire

01 Food & Beverage

Flavour, with feeling.

Our food & beverage business is built on the idea that a restaurant is more than its menu. It is the light, the room, the pace of service, the way a table is set — and the food, made without shortcuts.

Across our restaurants, bars and cafés, we aim for the same thing: places that feel relaxed but run with precision. Menus follow the season and the market; kitchens are led by people who care about craft.

What we do

  • Restaurants & cafés
  • Bars & lounges
  • Catering & private events
  • Concept & kitchen development

A good room is remembered long after the menu.

02 Hospitality

Stays with a
point of view.

Hospitality is where our food & beverage instincts meet a longer relationship: a night, a weekend, a season. We operate and curate stays where service is warm but never intrusive, and where the building does as much of the work as the staff.

Every stay is shaped by place — local materials, local food, local knowledge — with the standards of a group that runs its own kitchens and builds its own buildings.

What we do

  • Hotels & resorts
  • Villas & private stays
  • Guest experience & service design
  • Wellness & leisure

The details guests don't notice are the ones they remember.

03 Real Estate

Building for the
long term.

Real estate is the most permanent thing a group can do. We approach development as a long-term commitment to a place — land, architecture, construction quality and the life that follows occupancy.

AVORA Residences is the group's upcoming residential development and sets the template: restrained architecture, generous amenities, and a design language that will still feel considered in twenty years.

What we do

  • Residential development
  • Land & investment
  • Project delivery & construction management
  • Property & asset management
