03 Verticals

Seven companies.
One standard.

Engineering, construction, homes, hospitality, bottled water, trading and financial services — distinct businesses, held to a shared idea of quality.

01 The businesses

Different trades. The same expectations.

Each company has its own rhythm: a construction programme is measured in months, a restaurant in nights, a water line in bottles, a loan in repayment cycles.

What connects them is a way of working — respect, transparency and collaboration — and the group's willingness to invest for the long term. Our companies are run separately, but they learn from each other.

Sahan Engineering

01 Engineering

Engineering that
holds up.

Sahan Engineering is the group's engineering and construction arm, registered with the ICTAD (C-9571) at Grade C-4 for building works, with additional grades for highways, bridges, water supply and drainage, irrigation, dredging and marine construction.

Our teams deliver buildings, roads, bridges, water infrastructure and marine works for government bodies and private clients — from airport cargo terminals and water treatment plants to schools, banks and defence facilities.

What we do

  • Building construction
  • Roads, highways & bridges
  • Water supply & drainage
  • Marine, storm water & reclamation
  • Renovation & fit-out

Sahan Construction

02 Construction

The main build.

Sahan Construction is the group's main real estate and construction company, formed from the original Sahan Engineering & Construction business. It handles building and civil works from first excavation to handover.

The portfolio runs from warehousing and factories to apartments, houses and government projects. The company also takes on government tenders, and works as a one-stop shop for clients who need design coordination, construction and finishing under one contract.

What we do

  • Commercial & industrial buildings
  • Residential & apartments
  • Government tenders
  • Warehousing & factories
  • Renovation & finishing

Twenty-five years of building, with more than 100 people in the group.

03 Sahan Homes

Homes, under
one roof.

Sahan Homes is the housing and apartment arm of Sahan Construction. It takes the group's building experience and applies it directly to homes — from land and feasibility through design, construction and handover.

Its first development is AVORA Residences in Gampaha: 24 apartments across 10 floors, with two, three and four-bedroom layouts, kids' and adult pools, a gym, a lounge, a sky garden and 24/7 security.

  • Land & feasibility

  • Sales & handover

A table with

05 Bottled water

Bottled at
source.

Pure Spring — Green Lanka Eco Science and Technologies — draws from a 115-metre underground aquifer and bottles at source in Bandarawela on a fully automated line. The water is natural mineral water, untouched between aquifer and bottle.

The range covers 500 ml, 1 litre, 1.5 litre, 5 litre and 19 litre bottles, with a premium range in development. The plant works to ISO 22000, SLS, HACCP and GMP standards, and the water is sold through supermarkets, shops, hotels and distributors across the island.

What we do

  • 500 ml – 19 L bottles
  • 19 L dispensers & refills
  • Premium range (in development)
  • Retail, wholesale & corporate supply
  • Home & office delivery

115-metre aquifer · Bandarawela factory · ISO 22000.

06 Trading

Nature within
reach.

Cassandara is the group's general trading company, registered in Dubai. It handles imports and exports, with fresh produce — vegetables in particular — at the core of the business.

The company sources, consolidates and ships produce between Sri Lanka, the Gulf and other markets, supplying wholesalers, retailers and hospitality buyers. Its Dubai registration gives the group a base for cross-border trade and a gateway into Gulf markets.

07 Financial services

Finance for
real life.

Invest Lanka is the group's financial services arm, offering loans and leasing to households and small businesses. The focus is straightforward: clear terms, fair valuations and repayments that fit the way people actually earn.

Products include personal loans, gold loans secured against jewellery, and leasing for three-wheelers and motorcycles — the vehicles that keep small businesses and families moving.
