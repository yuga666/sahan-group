01 Who we are

A group built around
the things people remember.

Different businesses, one standard of care — from the first plate served to the final detail of a build.

Sahan is a versatile group of companies spanning food & beverage, hospitality and real estate.

We are a Sri Lankan group built around the things people come back to: a table worth lingering at, a stay that feels considered, a home that holds its value. Our businesses sit close to daily life — and each is run with the same attention to detail.

Growth, for us, is measured in decades rather than quarters. We invest in people, places and craft, and we build slowly enough to build well.

AVORA is the group's upcoming residential development: a slender tower of private residences wrapped in timber, stone and glass. At its crown, a rooftop deck holds an infinity pool, a landscaped garden and a sky gym with the city at your feet.

Every residence opens to a balcony; every floor is planned for light, cross-breeze and privacy. It is a building made to be lived in slowly — and to age well.

04 How we work

The Sahan way.

Four principles run through every business we operate — quietly, and without exception.

01

Care in the detail

The last five percent is the part people feel. We plan for it, budget for it and refuse to rush it.

02

Long-term over fast

We would rather build something that lasts than something that lands quickly. Patience is part of the method.

03

Local knowledge

Sri Lankan context, materials and people sit at the centre of our decisions — not at the edges.

04

One standard

The same expectations apply in a kitchen, a lobby and on a construction site.
