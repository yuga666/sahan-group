01 Who we are

A group built around
what people depend on.

Seven companies, one standard — from a water source in Bandarawela to a skyline in Gampaha.

Sahan is a diversified group of companies spanning engineering and construction, homes, hospitality, water, trading and financial services.

We began in 2001 as a construction business in Gampaha. Twenty-five years later, the group builds roads, bridges, water systems, warehouses, homes and apartments — and runs restaurants, a bottling plant, a Dubai trading desk and a finance arm.

The businesses are different, but the standard is shared: respect for the people we work with, transparency in how we work, and collaboration across the group.

05 Commitments

Building with less,
for longer.

Four commitments that shape how we source, build and operate — applied across every company, reviewed each year.

01

Climate & energy

Efficient plant, lower-energy kitchens and building standards that reduce what a place asks for.

02

Local sourcing

Menus, markets and a water source that stay close to Sri Lanka — from farm to pass, and aquifer to bottle.

03

Materials & craft

Timber, stone and concrete chosen for longevity, repairability and local supply.

04

People & community

Training that leads somewhere, fair work, and neighbourhood partnerships that outlast a project.

These commitments are reviewed annually across the group's companies.

07 How we work

The Sahan way.

Four principles run through every company we operate — quietly, and without exception.

01

Respect

For clients, for the people on site and in the kitchen, and for the places we build in.

02

Transparency

Clear scopes, clear pricing and clear reporting — the same whether the client is a bank or a homeowner.

03

Collaboration

Our companies work as one group. A project can draw on engineering, construction, homes and hospitality together.

04

Long-term over fast

We would rather build something that lasts than something that lands quickly. Patience is part of the method.
